# Institutional facts used by the Trust Passport

Every claim in this file is a checkable fact about how Indian institutions
actually operate. The Trust Passport cites these when it fails a caller's
claimed identity, which is why the wording states what the institution *does*
rather than what a scammer might say.

## No agency conducts a "digital arrest"

There is no provision in Indian law for arrest, custody, or interrogation over
a video or voice call. "Digital arrest" is not a legal procedure — the phrase
exists only in the scam script. Police, CBI, ED, NCB and customs conduct
arrests in person, with documentation, and never require the subject to stay
on a video call, remain alone in a room, or avoid contacting family.

A caller who says the subject is "under digital arrest", must "keep the video
on", or may not "disconnect or the case gets worse" has, by that sentence
alone, identified the call as fraudulent.

## Banks and the RBI never ask for OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords

No bank, wallet, payment app, or the Reserve Bank of India will ever ask a
customer to share a one-time password, card PIN, UPI PIN, CVV, or net-banking
password — not for verification, not to reverse a fraudulent transaction, not
to "cancel" anything. Bank staff can already see everything about the account
they legitimately need to see.

Any request for these values is fraudulent regardless of how the caller
identifies themselves or how urgent they claim the situation is.

## The RBI holds no personal customer accounts

The RBI is the central bank. It does not maintain accounts for individuals,
does not hold "verification" or "supervised" or "escrow" accounts into which
citizens transfer their savings while an investigation proceeds, and does not
collect fines, court fees, or bail from members of the public.

"Transfer your funds to an RBI supervised account for verification" describes
an arrangement that does not exist.

## Money is never seized by asking the owner to send it

No investigating agency asks a suspect or witness to voluntarily transfer
money to any account for safekeeping, verification, or clearance. Asset
seizure follows a court order and is executed against the institution holding
the asset — never by asking the account holder to make a transfer themselves.

Any framing in which the subject must *send* money to prove innocence, secure
a refund, release a parcel, or clear their name is a payment-setup script.

## TRAI does not disconnect numbers for criminal cases

TRAI is a regulator of telecom operators. It has no power to block an
individual subscriber's number, and it does not phone subscribers about
misuse of their SIM, Aadhaar-linked connections, or illegal activity. Calls
opening with "your number will be disconnected in two hours by TRAI" are a
fixed opening line of the courier and digital-arrest scripts.

## Real case numbers are not read out over the phone as proof

FIR numbers, case IDs, and badge numbers offered unprompted early in a call
are a credibility prop, not evidence. A genuine FIR can be verified
independently through the state police portal or by visiting the station named
— and a genuine officer will have no objection to the subject doing exactly
that before continuing the conversation.

## The 1930 helpline and cybercrime.gov.in

India's national cybercrime helpline is **1930**, and complaints can be filed
at **cybercrime.gov.in**. Reporting a fraudulent transfer within the first
few hours materially improves the chance of the funds being frozen before
they are withdrawn — the "golden hour" of the reporting chain.

This is the number to give someone who has already sent money. It is also the
answer to "what do I do now", which is the question that follows every
successful intervention.
