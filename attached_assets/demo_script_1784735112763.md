# KAVACH Demo Script

**Total Time: ~3-4 Minutes**

## 1. Introduction (30 seconds)
- **Speaker:** "Hi judges. Every day, millions are lost to sophisticated cyber-scams — fake customs officials, digital arrests, and tech support fraud. Scammers use fear, urgency, and isolation."
- **Speaker:** "Current tools only help *after* the money is gone. Today, we present **KAVACH**: an AI-powered system designed to intervene *before* the victim transfers any money."

## 2. The Citizen Experience: Live Protection (1.5 minutes)
- **Action:** Open the **Live Protection** tab in the web app. Click "Try a demo call" (to ensure a flawless presentation without relying on live room audio/microphone issues).
- **Speaker:** "Imagine I'm a citizen. I just got a call from someone claiming to be FedEx. They say a package in my name was intercepted with illegal goods, and they are transferring me to the 'Cyber Police'."
- **Action:** Let the demo audio play. Point out the UI elements as they react.
- **Speaker:** "Notice how KAVACH is listening and analyzing locally. The **Threat Meter** instantly escalates from 'Calm' to 'Watch' to 'Critical'. It detects the 'Authority Impersonation' tactic."
- **Action:** Highlight the coaching text on the screen.
- **Speaker:** "Most importantly, KAVACH coaches the victim. It tells them: *'This is a known scam tactic. Hang up immediately. Real police will never ask for money or OTPs over the phone.'* It breaks the isolation."

## 3. The Analyst/Authority Experience: Investigation (1 minute)
- **Action:** When the call ends, show how it transitions seamlessly into the **Investigation Report**.
- **Speaker:** "Once the call is over — or if the victim uploads a screenshot or recording — the data flows into our Investigation Report."
- **Action:** Scroll through the report. Point out the deterministic scoring, the coercion graph, and the extracted entities (phone numbers, bank accounts).
- **Speaker:** "Our AI engine isn't a black-box LLM. It's a deterministic, multi-stage classifier. It assigns specific scores to coercion and fear tactics. This means the results are reproducible and defensible in court."

## 4. Scalability & Threat Intelligence (30 seconds)
- **Action:** Switch to the **Scam Map** tab.
- **Speaker:** "All this data feeds into our live intelligence map. Authorities can see real-time hotspots of specific scam types across the country. They can cluster reports and track the scammers' infrastructure."

## 5. Conclusion (15 seconds)
- **Speaker:** "KAVACH isn't just about awareness; it's about active, real-time defense. We're turning every citizen's device into a shield against cyber-fraud. Thank you."
